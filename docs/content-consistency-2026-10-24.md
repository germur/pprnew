# Editorial consistency — October 24, 2026

## Content standard

- 24/7 means contact availability. A request does not confirm dispatch, arrival, a same-day visit or a repair schedule.
- Assessment and the agreed scope define restoration work, progress checks and specialist responsibilities. Do not promise universal daily attendance, complete material recovery or permanent mold prevention.
- Project records support discussion with the insurer. They do not determine coverage, approval, payment or a deductible-only obligation. Billing is an arrangement to confirm for the project.
- Mold assessment, sampling, laboratory analysis, remediation and post-remediation assessment have distinct roles. Do not infer independence from use of a separate laboratory or infer health safety from a sample result.
- Project photographs describe observed conditions and their real city; do not label a photograph as proof of a loss cause or successful service without supporting records.
- Ratings, reviews, named experts and technical review claims require an identified source. The unused 5.0/16 rating placeholders and the unsubstantiated article review attribution were removed. Established business credentials were retained from the shared company data; this change does not independently verify them or add professional licence claims.

## Implementation

Home, Commercial and Our Process share one five-step model. Service-specific methods remain contextual. InsuranceSupport and ServiceFacts replace conflicting repeated sales blocks and unsupported metrics on the five service pages. The insurance page now explains project records and responsibilities instead of implying the website opens a claim or guarantees payment.

FAQ text and FAQPage JSON-LD are generated from the same questions and answers. Shared metadata and city-page introductions no longer promise immediate dispatch or claim approval. Unique geographic context and neighbourhood lists remain; this is not a new audit of every local geographic statement.

The mold article now links to primary public information and displays an editorial update date instead of an unidentified expert review. Service gallery blocks now use approved project examples with observational descriptions; no new photos or customer information were imported.
